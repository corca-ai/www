Corca selected as OpenAI Select Partner

Corca was selected as an OpenAI Select Partner.

Corca, which signed an OpenAI Services Partner contract last February, was announced as an OpenAI Select Partner with the launch of the OpenAI Partner Network on July 14. nowCorca's official profile on the OpenAI Partner NetworkYou can also check out Corca here.

This selection is the result of Corca's experience in products and customer sites leading to official partner activities. Corca creates and operates AI products for researchers, while also working in the field to help companies utilize AI in their actual work.

Image of partnership between OpenAI and Corca

Corca has built and operated its own AI products.

Corca is a team that has been building and operating AI products. one of themMoonlighthelps researchers read and understand papers more quickly and deeplyAI Research FellowIt is. The experience of solving stuck concepts, highlighting key points, and continuing citations and notes while reading a paper has been incorporated into the product.

Operating a product is not just about looking at the performance of the model. We've continued to learn what users trust, when they find help, and what it takes to make good features a daily habit. Moonlight is a starting point for Corca's approach to AI.

Moonlight product screen. An AI research colleague who helps researchers read and understand papers.

We have expanded our product experience to the AX field.

Corca has also been working with companies on their AI transformation (AX) based on the experience gained from the product. We met with teams from different industries, including finance, proptech, and education, and thought about the process of AI becoming established within each company's way of working.

Corporate AI adoption is not summarized in one answer. Therefore, Corca has accumulated experience in a way that suits each organization's situation, from training, cultivating in-house AI champions, introducing ChatGPT Enterprise, and building customized AI solutions and AI-native teams. This range of offerings is also introduced in Corca's profile on the OpenAI Partner Network.
