This is an interview conducted to inform the public about the work that the Corca Moonlight Team does.

Sunyoung Kim (HR Manager) asked, and Yerin Kim (Growth Marketer) answered.

  • What is the atmosphere and culture of the Corca Moonlight Team?
  • What does a Growth Marketer do and what concerns do they have?

Q. Please introduce yourself!

Hello, my name is Yerin Kim and I work as a Growth Marketer on the Moonlight team!

Q. Please tell us about what you do at Corca!

I am in charge of Moonlight’s overall marketing. As a growth marketer, I broadly deal with the AARRR funnel, which is commonly referred to as a growth marketer, and carry out various marketing activities for the growth of Moonlight, from user inflow to activation and retention.

What is AARRR?

AARRR is a concept that organizes the entire process, starting from product awareness, through activation, retention, profit, and finally friend recommendation, into one funnel.

So at each stage of this funnel ISee where the problem is and find a way to solve the problem through marketingI'm doing what I'm doing.

Q. Did you encounter any difficulties while joining Corca as its first Growth Marketer?

It was difficult because some parts of the system were not yet in place. I thought I would be good at it because I thought I liked planning and building things up on my own, but the process of building things from zero, one by one, turned out to be not as easy as I thought.

Fortunately, my team members helped me a lot. In the process, I realized that the role of a growth marketer is not a person solely responsible for marketing, but rather a member of a growth team with developers and designers, each with their own role.

While having coffee chat again, something I saw before came to mind. The story was that even if the company does not have a CEO, an employee of a competitor in the building next door can also become a CEO. So, I think I relieved a lot of my frustration by having coffee chats with various team members regarding marketing.

AdditionallyAI is also a good teacher for meThis is being done. Before coming to the company, I had barely used AI, but Corca actively supports the use of AI at the company level, so I naturally started using AI a lot while working. In the process, I am receiving great help in quickly organizing issues I was worrying about or finding direction.

This is something I have been working on since joining the company, butConfidence is the most importantI think it does. Selling it well is important, but it is just as important.I need the power to make my team listen to my story and persuade them.I think it does.

Especially as I was working in my first position, I felt that it was important to have the mindset that my opinion could become a standard and the confidence that comes with it. These aspects seem to be essential to working as a Growth Marketer.

Q. When do you feel the fun and reward of work? Conversely, when is work difficult and difficult?

When you feel fun and rewarded from workThe process of defining a problem and finding a breakthroughI think it is. For example, Moonlight is trying both top-down and bottom-up approaches to connect with schools, and in the process,How can we create demand for our products?I thought about it a lot.

As a result, we were able to approach the student council first through a bottom-up approach, and with better results than expected, we were able to hold a large number of related partnership events. like thisWhen you find a new way to solve a problemI think I had the most fun.

The recent university partnership challenge received great participation. I’m curious what results and insights you gained through this challenge. Are there any challenges you have planned next?

Speaking of achievements, we didn't expect sales, and I think the biggest achievement was that 2,700 users learned about Moonlight.

In addition to those who applied, I think it was a good campaign in that Moonlight was exposed to more than 2,700 people through various media outlets, including Everytime, and was able to secure a certain level of awareness.

Through my insights, I realized that Moonlight is actually helpful to students and that it is a product that people are using because they need it.

I think Moonlight is a product that is somewhat influenced by the March semester season, so I'm thinking about holding an event before the start of the semester.

In particular, looking at existing data, the activity and retention tend to be higher in cases where users are invited through friend invitations than when they are not. So, I'm thinking that we could plan an event in the lab or by inviting friends from school.

Based on the experience gained through this university partnership challenge, we also believe that Moonlight could be a starting point to try a fun and larger-scale event.
