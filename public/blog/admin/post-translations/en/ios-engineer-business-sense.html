Q. Please introduce yourself!

Hello, my name is Seonghoon Kim and I am working as an iOS Engineer. nice to meet you!

Q. Please tell us about what you do at Corca!

I am providing a schedule management app called Trace. Trace is a service that helps users manage their schedules more easily and quickly by utilizing LLM. I would definitely recommend this to anyone interested in schedule management! (I highly recommend it to Js!)

Are there any features you recommend in Trace?

Trace is an LLM-based communication service, so its biggest advantage is that you can quickly register schedules just by capturing them, for example, using the shortcut function using images. Recently, a voice recording function is also scheduled to be released soon, and I think you will be very satisfied if you try it yourself.

Q. What capabilities or mindset do you think is needed to do a good job as an iOS Engineer at Corca?

Of course, technical capabilities as an iOS engineer are basic,A look at services from a business perspectiveI think it's also important. And since this is not something you do alone, but a service created by a team, I think it is really important to be considerate of your colleagues in order for everyone to work towards the same goal.

Q. When do you feel the fun and reward of work? Conversely, when is work difficult and difficult?

While operating Service Trace, I feel the greatest reward when I receive positive feedback from users, such as “I’m using it well” and “The update was really good.” I feel very proud that the service I created is actually helping someone's life. On the other hand, I tend to get a bit stressed when a task I'm in charge of doesn't turn out as well as I expected or when I feel like there might be a disruption to my schedule. But every time that happens, my teammates say, “It’s okay, let’s do it together” and help me, which is a great help💪

The area that has grown the most isLooking at problems from a service and business perspective rather than a developer perspectiveIt is. In the past, I was stuck in the mindset of a developer, but after joining the Trace team, I began to think first of all, “How will this help users?” and I think I was able to naturally develop a business perspective by quickly discussing and reflecting with the team.

Q. What topic does Korca's Trace team talk about most often these days?

Which countries are you discussing entering into?

Currently, Trace's service is distributed worldwide, but there is a lot of talk about localizing it to be more language and culture-specific. So, recently, we have been discussing how to approach it in a way that can better penetrate users in specific countries such as Japan, China, and India.
