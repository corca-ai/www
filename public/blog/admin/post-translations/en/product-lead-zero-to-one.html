This is an interview conducted to inform the public about what the Corca Product Team does.

Sunyoung Kim (HR Manager) and Minchae Kim (Product Manager) asked, and Jaeho Jeong (Product Lead) answered.

  • What is the atmosphere and culture of the Corca Product Team?
  • What does a Product Lead do and what concerns do they have?
  • What does the product lead dream of for Corca?

Q. Please introduce yourself!

My name is Jaeho Jeong, and I joined Corca as a Product Manager in December of last year, and am currently in charge of Product Team Lead and LLM Native squad Lead!

Q. Please tell us about what you do at Corca!

Now mainly0 to 1 work from product ideation to bringing it to marketI'm doing it.Verify PMFIf I'm doing it and it doesn't work, I pivot the product and repeat!

What is PMF?

Simply put, Product Market Fit is the compatibility between the product and the market. PMF is important because you have to release a product that is in demand in the market!

Please tell us what kind of Trace app you are currently developing!

Trace is a memo app based on LLM.This is it. It is not limited to a specific function, and if it is a notification, it is a notification, if it is a thought, it is a thought, if it is a to do, it is a to do.If you record freely, it will be systematically saved by LLM.It's okay. While existing memo apps only allow keyword-based searches, Trace allows context-based searches. It is an LLM-based memo app that has various functions, such as answering when you ask, "Why am I like this recently?" and automatically setting a reminder when you ask, "Set a reminder for 5 o'clock tomorrow!"

Want to know more about trace?

My own AI memo, trace, that makes everyday life easier

As for the LLM Native squad,“Let’s work like a solo entrepreneur”This is the tone of our squad. I think it's fun to quickly implement what you've planned and see the outline of the results. Q. What was the area where you grew the most while working as a Product Manager at Corca?

I usually read a lot of product-related books and YouTube videos, and I feel like I've grown when I can understand the theories physically by trying things I only understood theoretically at Corca. The saying “Let’s work Lean” is famous in startups, but I think I have grown when I take it as meaning only, but when I actually work Lean and feel, ‘Ah, that’s what I meant’.

Q. What is the most frequently talked about topic in Korca's LLM native squad these days?

I think **“Conversation with Customers”** is what we talk about the most. If you don't consciously try to communicate with customers, you will be biased toward what you want, and you will end up creating products that are only conscious of your own tastes and preferences, not PMF's, so I think I'm talking about having more conversations with customers.
